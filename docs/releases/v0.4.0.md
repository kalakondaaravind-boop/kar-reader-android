## KAR READER 0.4.0 — development build

Better transcription for **Hindi and Hinglish**, and a choice of **Devanagari or Roman** letters for Hindi.
Tested on an Android emulator with real recordings; not yet tried on a physical phone.

### Which file to download
- **KAR-READER-0.4.0.apk** — the standard, fully offline app (no network permission at all). Start with this one.
- **KAR-READER-Sync-0.4.0.apk** — the same app plus direct Wi-Fi sync between your own devices.

Each is about 640 MB because all speech models are inside the app, so nothing is ever downloaded.

### What's new
- **Automatic mode recognizes English, Hindi and Hinglish.** Each piece of a recording is identified as English or
  Hindi and sent to the model made for it: Whisper for English, Omnilingual ASR for Hindi and Hinglish.
- **Other languages** are transcribed only when you pick them in the language list (Omnilingual ASR, 1600+ languages).
- **Write Hindi in Devanagari or Roman letters** ("har koi duniya ko badalne ki sochta hai"). Switch any time from
  the transcript screen; exports and notes follow your choice. No re-transcribing needed.

### Checked on the emulator
- Hindi test sentence, automatic: "हर कोई दुनिया को बदलने की सोचता है लेकिन कोई खुद को बदलने की सोचता ही नहीं" (exact).
- Two English speakers: separated correctly, one small word error ("rights" for "writes").
- German chosen by hand: transcribed in German. German left on Automatic comes out as English, as designed.

### Known limits
- Hindi and other non-English text has no punctuation and is all lowercase (Omnilingual ASR writes it that way).
- Roman Hindi is converted letter by letter, so English words spoken inside Hindi may be spelled as they sound.
- Speed and accuracy on real phones, noisy rooms and long recordings are not yet measured.

### Installing over 0.3.x
0.3.x was signed with a one-off key, so Android won't install 0.4.0 over it. Uninstall the old version first
(**this deletes its data**: back up books and notes in Settings › Backup first; recordings aren't in backups yet).
