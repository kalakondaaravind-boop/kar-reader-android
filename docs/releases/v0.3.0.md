## KAR READER 0.3.0 — development build

**Not yet tested on a phone or tablet.** It compiles and its automated tests pass; please treat it as a preview.

### Which file to download
- **KAR-READER-0.3.0.apk** — the standard, fully offline app (no network permission at all). Start with this one.
- **KAR-READER-Sync-0.3.0.apk** — the same app plus direct Wi-Fi sync between your own devices. Installs alongside.

Both are large (about 250–300 MB) because the speech models for transcription are inside the app, so nothing is ever downloaded.

### New: Transcribe
- Record a lecture, interview or meeting, or import an audio file (MP3, M4A, WAV, OGG, FLAC, AMR, MP4 audio).
- Each voice is labelled Speaker 1, 2, 3… — rename them, or merge two the app wrongly split.
- 99 languages with automatic detection (Whisper base, multilingual).
- Tap a line to hear it; touch and hold to correct text or speaker.
- Export as text, Markdown, SRT subtitles or PDF; save as a note linked to a book.
- Runs entirely on the device. Deleted transcripts go to the recycle bin for 30 days.

### Known limits
- Speakers are labelled after recording stops, not live.
- Transcription is slow on phones (it may take about as long as the recording) and accuracy varies by language,
  noise and microphone distance. Overlapping speech and similar voices can be mixed up.
- Transcripts are not yet included in backups or sync.

### How to install
1. Download the APK on your Android device (Android 8.0 or newer).
2. Open it and allow "Install unknown apps" for your browser or file manager when asked.
3. Open KAR READER.
